Notes for translators
POTENTIAL SPOILER WARNING
FOR QUESTS AND SECRETS!!!
-------------------------

A small note on if you're translating the phantom's (Ira) ability names to a language which uses non-english characters: the font the phantom uses in-game currently doesn't support non-english characters and I don't know how to allow it to support them

`hardmod_anvil_dir_*` are directions used in `hardmod_anvil_book_moved_desc`

Book names like "Obseratum" and "Restitutio in Integrum" are latin

Ensure all biome modifier names (`hardmod_biomemodifier_name_*`) are unique, they're used in status effect names and a biome can have multiple modifiers at once, so they can't be duplicated

`hardmod_book_ending_hint_desc` is referencing "Flummoxium" and "Unstable Teleportatium" making "Guiding Powder", which points to the nearest orb

`hardmod_book_secret_lab_path_desc` is referencing a new mini-quest in the mod, where you can bring Ukkoskivi (electric stone), Kiuaskivi (fire stone), Sauvan Ydin (tinker stone) and Petrified Cheese (hardmod's item) on the mountain altar for perks
